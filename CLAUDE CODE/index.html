Rooted in Tradition. Crafted for the Modern Palate.

We travel across the islands of Indonesia to bring you ethically sourced beans, honoring the generations of farmers who cultivate our nation's rich soil.

Every lot is traced to a single village, a single elevation, a single harvest window — then roasted in small batches so the character of the land arrives intact.
